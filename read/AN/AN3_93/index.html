Urgent

14문장·0 / 14 읽음
0%
S1

There are these three urgent duties of a farming householder. Which three?

S2

There is the case where a farming householder quickly gets his field well-plowed & well-harrowed.

S3

Having quickly gotten his field well-plowed & well-harrowed, he quickly plants the seed.

S4

Having quickly planted the seed, he quickly lets in the water & then lets it out.

S5

These are the three urgent duties of a farming householder.

S6

Now, that farming householder does not have the power or might (to say:) 'May my crops spring up today, may the grains appear tomorrow, and may they ripen the next day.'

S7

But when the time has come, the farming householder's crops spring up, the grains appear, and they ripen.

S8

In the same way, there are these three urgent duties of a monk. Which three?

S9

The undertaking of the training in heightened virtue, the undertaking of the training in heightened mind, the undertaking of the training in heightened discernment.

S10

These are the three urgent duties of a monk.

S11

Now, that monk does not have the power or might (to say:) 'May my mind be released from effluents through lack of clinging/sustenance today or tomorrow or the next day.'

S12

But when the time has come, his mind is released from effluents through lack of clinging/sustenance.

S13

Thus, monks, you should train yourselves: 'Strong will be our desire for the undertaking of the training in heightened virtue. Strong will be our desire for the undertaking of the training in heightened mind. Strong will be our desire for the undertaking of the training in heightened discernment.'

S14

That's how you should train yourselves.
